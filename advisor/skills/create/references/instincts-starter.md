## Pre-Communication Filters

*Run these before any important communication in this domain.*

### The Meta-Filter
> Before any message, ask: **"Am I doing this for them (giving them what they need) or for me (managing my anxiety, proving my competence, seeking validation)?"** If it's for me, pause and rewrite.

### Match Language to Certainty
> Before asserting something, ask: **"How certain am I? And what's the cost of being wrong?"**

- 95%+ certainty = state it as fact
- 60-80% certainty = "I think / my investigation suggests"
- Below 60% = ask, don't assert
